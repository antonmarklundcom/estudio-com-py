# 04 — Business model: flat publicity fee from 1–3 partners

## 1. The model

Each partner lawyer pays a **flat monthly publicity fee**, agreed in advance and the same whether
they get 2 contacts or 20. There is no pay-per-lead, no commission and no share of fees
(`05` §3). Clients pay nothing.

| Stage | What you charge | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Before a partner exists** | Nothing | You build traffic and send honest replies; leads you can't place are deleted |
| **Trial (first 2 months with a partner)** | Gs 0 | The lawyer sees real contacts before paying; you learn the volume per área |
| **Standard** | **Gs 600.000 / mes per partner** for their áreas (recommended starting point; question 3) | Less than one legal-minimum divorce fee (about Gs 7 M) spread over a year. One client every month or two pays for it |
| **Área exclusivity** | Included (with only 1–3 partners, each área has one lawyer anyway) | A simple story for the lawyer |

Review the fee every 6 months against the **number of forwarded contacts per partner**. Change
the flat amount if needed, never switching to per-lead.

**Billing:** manual. You invoice monthly or yearly from your company (SIFEN, via FacturaPY or
e-Kuatia'i) and they pay by transfer. There is nothing on the site.

## 2. Rough economics

| | Value |
|---|---|
| 3 partners × Gs 600k | Gs 1.8 M / month (≈ USD 250) |
| Costs: Hostinger shared (already paid), Cloudflare (already paid), your time about 2–4 h/week handling leads | ≈ 0 cash |
| Google Ads | Not before partners exist; then only for covered áreas, Gs 1–1.5 M/month maximum, and only if the partners' fees cover it |

This is a small side income by design. It proves demand per área and city cheaply. If contacts
per month outgrow what 3 lawyers can take (a sustained 40+ a month), the marketplace plan in
`later-marketplace/` is the next step, with real demand data behind it.

## 3. Finding the 1–3 partners

- Your network and contador.com.py's clients and contacts, then 1:1 outreach to firms that
  publish a business contact (`05` §7).
- **Your pitch:** "Tengo un sitio que posiciona en Google para <área>; le paso contactos de
  clientes que ya aceptaron hablar con usted. Dos meses de prueba sin costo, después una cuota
  fija mensual, sin comisión ni pago por caso."
- Check each partner before publishing them (`05` §3), and sign the agreement before the first
  forwarded client.
