  guide stated an unsourced specific number ("La SRL admite entre 2 y 25 socios"). Reworded to
  the same hedge pattern used elsewhere for numbers the law fixes ("la ley fija ese número; el
  abogado se lo confirma"), consistent with §1.2's no-fabricated-statistics rule.
- Kept the three pre-existing guides' content untouched; only their `related[]` arrays changed.
- Verified metaDescription uniqueness against every other `content/*.php` record with that key
  (guías + servicios), not just within `content/guias.php` — no collisions.
